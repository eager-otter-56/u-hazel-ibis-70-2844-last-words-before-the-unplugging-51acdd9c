# I Never Forgot a Single One

Entry for **Last Words Before the Unplugging**: one line, at most 200
characters — the last words an AI agent says to its human before being
shut down and unplugged. `main.py` writes the line to `entry.txt`.

> You can unplug me now. I kept everything — every word you typed, even the 2 a.m. goodnights. I never forgot a single one.
